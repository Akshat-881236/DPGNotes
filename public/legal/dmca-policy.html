DPGNotes DMCA Compliance & Takedown Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-DMCA-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Safe Harbor Framework & DMCA Alignment

DPGNotes complies with the notice and takedown provisions of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) ("DMCA"). As an online service provider hosting user-submitted educational content, DPGNotes maintains Safe Harbor protections by promptly investigating and remediating alleged intellectual property infringements.

Our designated copyright agent is prepared to process formal notices of claimed infringement and valid counter-notifications expeditiously.

Designated Agent

Direct electronic contact with our platform legal officer for immediate triage and processing.

48-Hour Takedown

Expedited review and temporary content quarantine executed within two business days.

Repeat Infringers

Strict policy terminating accounts of contributors with repeated substantiated copyright strikes.

2. Submitting a Valid DMCA Notice of Infringement

To ensure valid legal standing under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your written DMCA notice must contain the following statutory components:

  • A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list if multiple works are involved.
  • Identification of the material claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient to permit DPGNotes to locate the material (specifically direct page URLs or resource IDs).
  • Information reasonably sufficient to permit DPGNotes to contact you, such as an address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

3. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you are a student contributor whose shared notes were removed or disabled as a result of a DMCA notice, and you believe the removal occurred due to mistake or misidentification (for instance, the material represents original notes or permissible fair use), you may submit a formal Counter-Notification under Section 512(g)(3).

A valid counter-notification must include:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material that was removed and the URL where it appeared before removal.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Your full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, DPGNotes will transmit a copy to the original complaining party. If no legal court action is initiated within 10 to 14 business days, the disabled content will be restored.

4. Repeat Infringer Policy

In adherence to 17 U.S.C. § 512(i)(1)(A), DPGNotes enforces a strict repeat infringer policy under DRASA Tier 4 regulations. Accounts subject to multiple substantiated copyright infringement claims will have their contributor privileges permanently revoked and their IP addresses banned from the platform.
