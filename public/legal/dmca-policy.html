DPGNotes DMCA Policy & Takedown Notice Procedure

Effective Date: July 13, 2026 | Document ID: POL-DPG-DMCA-2026-V3

1. Notice and Takedown Procedure (17 U.S.C. § 512)

DPGNotes respects copyright owners and has adopted safe harbor provisions consistent with 17 U.S.C. § 512 of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). If you believe your copyrighted work has been improperly shared on our portal, please submit a formal DMCA Notice.

2. Elements of a Valid DMCA Notice

A legally compliant DMCA notice must include:

  1. Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
  2. Specific identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. The exact URL (e.g. dpgnotes.web.app/dpgnotes-pdf-viewer.html?id=...) or unique document ID.
  4. Your contact information including full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and email.
  5. A statement of good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent, or the law.
  6. A statement made under penalty of perjury that the notification is accurate and authorized.

3. Counter-Notification Procedure

If a contributor believes their upload was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification, they may file a written counter-notification containing their signature, identification of the removed file, and consent to legal jurisdiction.

4. Designated Copyright Agent

Agent: Copyright Compliance Officer, DPGNotes

Organization: Akshat Network Hub

Submission Channel: Official Takedown & Grievance Form

Turnaround SLA: Actioned within 24 to 48 business hours of receipt.
