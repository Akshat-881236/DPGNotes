1. What Are Cookies and Browser Storage Technologies?

Cookies and browser LocalStorage are standardized web technologies that allow websites to store small data strings on your device. These packets ensure seamless user authentication, preserve visual interface preferences across page reloads, and facilitate anonymous visitor telemetry without requiring repetitive credential inputs.

This Cookie Policy explains how DPGNotes (maintained by Akshat Network Hub) uses cookies, local browser storage objects, and related tracking technologies in strict accordance with the ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC as amended), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India).

3. Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry Cookies

Our platform integrates Google AdSense and native sponsored partner units. Third-party advertising partners may set anonymous tracking cookies to:

  • Serve contextual advertisements relevant to academic disciplines and career pathways.
  • Limit the frequency with which a visitor is shown the exact same promotional banner.
  • Detect invalid click behavior and combat click-fraud attempts in real time.

You can opt out of personalized third-party ad profiling via Google Ad Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice tool.

4. Step-by-Step Guide to Managing & Clearing Cookies

Every major web browser empowers you to review, restrict, or clear stored cookies and local storage tokens. Follow the instructions corresponding to your browser:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Clear browsing data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Note: Disabling strictly essential cookies will prevent authenticated contributors from maintaining active sessions in the dashboard.
