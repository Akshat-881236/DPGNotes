DPGNotes DPGCoins Governance & Virtual Economy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-COINS-GOV-2026-V1.0 Version: 1.0 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Virtual Utility Charter & Non-Fiat Recognition

DPGNotes integrates an internal, closed-loop virtual utility token known as DPGCoins. The primary objective of DPGCoins is to sustain high-speed open-source educational computing, distribute bandwidth fairly, incentivize original academic peer publishing, deter aggressive automated crawling/scraping, and safeguard user account security across multiple devices.

DPGCoins constitute platform-exclusive utility points. They possess no fiat currency equivalent, cannot be cashed out, redeemed, or traded for real-world currencies, and exist solely as digital accounting entries within the DPGNotes cloud infrastructure.

2. Central Admin Treasury Routing Architecture

To preserve complete economic integrity, mathematical solvency, and centralized oversight across the platform, all virtual currency inflows and outflows are governed by sovereign routing rules:

100% Central Admin Treasury Inflow Routing

Every single coin deducted or penalized from any Guest or Contributor vault—including nominal page navigation tariffs, PDF reading milestones, manual session termination penalties, and regulatory breach assessments—is routed directly and securely into the Central Platform Admin Treasury account (its.akshatnetworkhub23@gmail.com). No intermediary accounts, burned balances, or unallocated pools exist.

Conversely, all welcome grants, authentic sign-in bonuses, academic publishing bounties, quiz submission rewards, and AI training bonuses are sovereignly minted and disbursed from this administrative authority.

User accounts and guest sessions are provisioned with dedicated cloud-backed or local cryptographic vaults:

4. Scope Page Access, Nominal Deductions & 4-Hour IndexedDB Window

Accessing content within the DPGNotes platform is subject to a nominal maintenance deduction of -20 DPGCoins. To ensure fair access and prevent repeated charging during standard academic study sessions, the platform enforces client-side IndexedDB logging (resource_visit_logs) with a strict 4-hour exemption window:

  • Standard Platform Pages: Identified by pathname (e.g. page::/dashboard.html). Visiting incurs -20 DPGCoins once per 4 hours.
  • PDF Educational Resources: Identified by composite canonical key: pdf::[DocID]::[Direct_PDF_URL]. Switching from one PDF to another incurs -20 DPGCoins. Returning to the exact same PDF resource within 4 hours is logged as a duplicate visit and incurs zero deduction.
  • Assignment Solutions: Identified by canonical query parameter: solution_assign::[assign_id] (e.g. solution_assign::assign_1794721052637_c5p33). Navigating to a new assignment solution incurs -20 DPGCoins; returning to the same assignment within 4 hours is completely exempt.
  • Practical Solutions: Identified by canonical query parameter: solution_prac::[prac_id] (e.g. solution_prac::prac_1795020824041_bq1os). Navigating to a new practical solution incurs -20 DPGCoins; returning to the same practical solution within 4 hours is completely exempt.

7. Contributor Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Coin Exchange

Contributors can transfer DPGCoins to fellow verified contributors via Dashboard > Manage DPGCoins subject to strict anti-collusion guardrails:

P2P Transfer Safeguards & Quotas

1. Minimum Vault Balance: Senders must hold an active vault balance of at least 3,000 DPGCoins to unlock transfer capabilities.
2. Minimum Transfer Amount: 500 DPGCoins per transaction.
3. Maximum Transfer Limit: No single transaction may exceed 50% of the sender's available balance.
4. Recipient Validation: Recipient must be a registered, verified Contributor UID in good standing.

8. Multi-Device Management & Cryptographic Session Security

To prevent credential sharing, unauthorized proxying, and session hijacking, DPGNotes captures and maintains comprehensive device telemetry for every active Contributor login:

  • Logged Parameters: Device Name, Device Category (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet), Geographic Location, IP Address, Timestamp, and a cryptographically signed Session ID.
  • Dual-Validation State: Session IDs are synchronized simultaneously in Firestore active sessions and client-side IndexedDB.
  • Remote Session Revocation: Contributors can inspect all active and previous logins under Dashboard > Manage Devices. Revoking a previous device deletes its session token from Firestore; any subsequent request from that device fails server-side validation and deletes local session storage immediately.

9. Negative Threshold (-5,000 Coins) & Quarantine Recovery Center

DPGNotes permits a maximum overdraft threshold of -5,000 DPGCoins before platform enforcement activates:

Quarantine Enforcement & Recovery Threshold

When an active vault balance breaches -5,000 DPGCoins, all open-source reading pages, academic solutions, quizzes, and utilities enter automated lockdown and redirect the user to earnCoin.html.

• Regulatory Integrity Fine: Direct navigation or tampering with earnCoin.html without valid server-issued sessionId and contributorId/guestId parameters violates platform terms and incurs an immediate, non-negotiable -5,000 DPGCoins deduction.
• Recovery Requirement: The "Go To Destination" exit button remains locked until the user successfully earns at least +350 DPGCoins through arithmetic challenges, technical prompts, or contributor quiz modules.

10. Automated 15-Day Storage Lifecycle & Ledger Purge

To preserve database performance, minimize cloud storage overhead, and uphold strict user privacy standards, DPGNotes enforces an automated 15-day document lifecycle across all virtual economy logs:

  • Transaction Ledger (coins_transactions): All itemized credit and debit logs older than 15 days from their creation timestamp are permanently batched and purged from Firestore storage during server health routines.
  • Dispute Reports (coins_deduction_reports): All deduction dispute tickets and resolution records reaching 15 days of age are automatically deleted.
  • Currency Analytics: Administrator Currency Analytics displays only verified active transactions within the trailing 15-day rolling window.
