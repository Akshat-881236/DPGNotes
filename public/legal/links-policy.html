DPGNotes External Links Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-LINK-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Objective & The Outbound Interception Architecture

To protect students from malicious redirects, phishing domains, and unverified commercial traps, DPGNotes implements a global client-side External Link Interception Architecture. Whenever a user clicks an outbound hyperlink leading away from DPGNotes, our system intercepts the navigation and presents an informative warning modal.

This External Links Policy establishes our link verification standards, trusted domains whitelist, and user safety practices.

Interception Modal

Informs visitors before they exit the academic perimeter, displaying the full transparent destination URL.

Trusted Whitelist

Seamless navigation to verified internal partners including DigiIndia Student Search Engine.

Security Headers

Outbound links automatically apply rel="noopener noreferrer" to prevent reverse tab-jacking exploits.

2. The Trusted Internal Whitelist Architecture

To balance rigorous student safety with smooth navigation, DPGNotes maintains a programmatic Trusted Whitelist. Hyperlinks pointing to verified affiliated academic services bypass the external redirect warning and open directly. Our trusted whitelist includes:

  • dpgnotes.web.app and dpgnotes.firebaseapp.com — The primary official DPGNotes production portals.
  • digiindia-student-platform.onrender.com and digiindia — Our verified collaborative student search engine and partner directory.
  • localhost and 127.0.0.1 — Local development, educational testing, and offline preview environments.
  • Dynamic host matches for verified GitHub Pages deployments and official mirrors.

3. External Link Security Controls & Exploit Defense

When users choose to proceed to external web destinations, DPGNotes automatically enforces standardized browser security attributes:

  • rel="noopener": Prevents the newly opened third-party webpage from acquiring access to your current DPGNotes window object via window.opener, neutralizing reverse tab-jacking exploits.
  • rel="noreferrer": Protects your educational privacy by withholding the full referrer URL containing search queries or internal note identifiers.
  • Safe Browsing Checks: Contributor-submitted external links are cross-referenced against automated malware blacklists prior to publication.

4. No Endorsement & Disclaimer of Third-Party Liability

The appearance of external hyperlinks on DPGNotes does not constitute an endorsement, official sponsorship, or commercial recommendation of the external website, its sponsoring organization, or any commercial products advertised therein. DPGNotes holds no liability for external website availability, privacy practices, or content fidelity.

5. Reporting Deceptive or Broken Outbound Links

Students and contributors who discover malicious, broken, or deceptive external links within any lecture note card or author bio are urged to report the URL to dpgnotes.legal@gmail.com. Reported links are inspected and removed from our database within 24 hours.
