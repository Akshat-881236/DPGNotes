DPGNotes Advertising Policy & Community Standards

Effective Date: July 13, 2026 | Document ID: POL-DPG-ADS-2026-V3

1. Purpose & Supported Formats

DPGNotes enables students, developers, and educators to promote academic tools, open-source repositories, and verified tutorials across five formats: DPGNotes Resource Boost, LinkedIn Post, Medium Story, GitHub Repository, and YouTube Tech Video.

2. Advertising Quality & Safety Rules

All sponsored advertisements must comply with the following standards:

  • Academic Relevance: Campaigns must relate to computer science, engineering, management, sciences, or professional student development.
  • Direct Landing Endpoints: Advertisements must link directly to authentic, operational pages without misleading intermediary redirects.
  • No Academic Dishonesty: Promotion of essay-writing mills, contract cheating, exam proxy services, or unverified software cracks is strictly prohibited.

3. Native Ads System & Reader Page Restrictions

To preserve reading comfort and avoid ad clutter in the DPGNotes PDF Viewer, Native Ads are strictly disallowed in After Notes for pages divisible by 3 (Pages 3, 6, 9, 12...), as those slots are reserved for system sponsors.
