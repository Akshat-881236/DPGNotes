DPGNotes Comprehensive Advertising Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-ADVT-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Institutional Monetization Philosophy & Educational Charter

DPGNotes was founded on the fundamental principle that higher education resources, syllabus guides, curated lecture notes, formula sheets, and practical academic tools should remain universally accessible to all students regardless of economic background. To subsidize high-bandwidth cloud infrastructure, low-latency document rendering engines, zero-trust cryptographic security audits, and continuous content moderation without charging tuition fees or paywalling educational notes, DPGNotes employs a non-invasive, ethically curated advertising model.

This Advertising Policy governs all promotional messaging, automated programmatic inventory, native educational sponsorships, and interactive reward units deployed across DPGNotes. By utilizing the platform, users and advertisers acknowledge adherence to these rigorous operational standards.

AdSense Certified

Full conformance to Google Publisher Policies, strict transparency labels, and zero deceptive click mechanics.

Non-Intrusive UX

Strict observance of Better Ads Standards: no pop-ups, auto-play audio, forced countdowns, or intrusive interstitials.

Student Protection

Zero tolerance for academic dishonesty services, ghostwriting, predatory loans, gambling, or age-restricted content.

2. Google AdSense Publisher Program Compliance

DPGNotes partners with Google AdSense and accredited digital advertising networks to deliver contextual, non-disruptive advertisements. We enforce rigorous structural and algorithmic guardrails to preserve absolute compliance with Google Publisher Policies, including:

  • No Artificial Click Generation: DPGNotes strictly prohibits any automated bot interactions, incentivized clicks, or manual click rings. Users are reminded that clicking advertisements should solely occur out of genuine consumer interest.
  • Clear Structural Demarcation: Every programmatic banner is visibly labelled with unambiguous identifiers such as "Advertisement", "Sponsored", or "Promoted Resource", ensuring students never confuse editorial lecture notes with paid promotions.
  • Layout Isolation & Accidental Click Prevention: All interactive ad units are encased within dedicated bounding boxes featuring sufficient padding and distinct background contrast to eliminate accidental taps on mobile touchscreens.
  • Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) Mitigation: All ad slots possess hard-coded minimum CSS dimension reservations (`min-height` and `aspect-ratio`) to prevent sudden viewport jumping when banners asynchronously load.

4. Pedagogical Immersion & The Divisible-by-Three (Div-by-3) Architecture

Academic comprehension demands uninterrupted concentration. Reading complex algorithmic proofs, biochemical pathways, or jurisprudence case analyses requires unbroken visual focus. To prevent cognitive fatigue caused by ad oversaturation, DPGNotes created and enforces the Divisible-by-Three (Div-by-3) Rule across our digital PDF Reader and Note Viewer:

  • Pages whose numerical index is divisible by three (e.g., Page 3, Page 6, Page 9, Page 12) may host an official Google AdSense horizontal display unit.
  • Custom native sponsor cards and secondary ad units are strictly forbidden from rendering in the "After Note" slot of any page divisible by 3.
  • No more than one promotional container is permitted per 600 vertical words of editorial text, maintaining an industry-leading content-to-ad ratio exceeding 80:20.

5. Better Ads Standards & Prohibited Interactive Behaviors

DPGNotes complies strictly with the Coalition for Better Ads desktop and mobile environments. The following aggressive advertising tactics are permanently barred from our systems:

  • Pop-up and Pop-under Windows: No advertisement may launch external windows or obscure the primary browser viewport upon load or exit.
  • Prestitial and Countdown Blocking Overlays: Users are never forced to wait through mandatory countdown timers before viewing syllabus notes or downloading public examination papers.
  • Auto-Playing Sound or Video: Advertisements containing video or multimedia elements must be permanently muted by default. Unsolicited audio playback is considered a critical security violation.
  • Deceptive UI Mimicry: Banners imitating operating system alerts, simulated "Download" buttons, fake security warnings, or mock interactive chat windows are permanently banned.

6. Categorically Prohibited Advertising Verticals

In adherence to our student protection charter and institutional academic integrity guidelines, DPGNotes enforces an uncompromising ban on the following product and service categories:

  • Academic Dishonesty & Contract Cheating: Essay writing services, dissertation ghostwriting, proxy examination assistance, and commercially traded solved test banks.
  • Adult & Sexually Explicit Content: Pornography, escort services, sexually suggestive games, or age-restricted dating services.
  • Gambling, Betting & Speculative Financial Schemes: Real-money online casinos, sports betting platforms, unregulated binary options, and volatile crypto-token speculation.
  • Malware, Phishing & Unauthorized Utilities: Adware bundles, rogue browser toolbars, deceptive driver installers, or password cracking utilities.
  • Tobacco, Alcohol & Controlled Substances: Promotion of cigarettes, electronic vaping devices, alcoholic beverages, and pharmaceutical drugs requiring a prescription.
  • Predatory Financial Products: Unlicensed payday loan platforms, high-interest unsecured credit schemes, or deceptive student debt consolidation offers.

7. Protection of Minors & Children's Online Privacy

While DPGNotes is primarily structured for collegiate and vocational students, we acknowledge that secondary school students and minors may access academic formula sheets and syllabus guides. In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (Section 9 regarding Processing of Personal Data of Children) and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • DPGNotes strictly prohibits behavioral tracking, persistent profiling, and targeted behavioral advertising directed at known minors.
  • We request our programmatic ad partners to serve non-personalized, contextual-only advertising units whenever student demographic data indicates minor age status.
  • Advertisements marketing mature content, dating, weapons, or age-inappropriate commodities are blocked site-wide regardless of device location.

8. Advertising Cookies, Telemetry & Opt-Out Mechanisms

Third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense, utilize cookies, web beacons, and persistent device identifiers (e.g., __gads, __gpi) to measure ad impressions, combat click fraud, and serve personalized marketing based on past browsing history. You possess absolute authority to manage and terminate advertising cookies:

  • Google Ads Personalization: Customize or disable personalized ad profiles by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry Self-Regulation Platforms: Opt out of targeted behavioral advertising across hundreds of ad networks simultaneously via the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) and the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA).
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC) & Do Not Track: Our servers honor browser-level GPC signals, suppressing third-party tracking cookies automatically.

For exhaustive technical details regarding our cookie storage tables and retention windows, consult our comprehensive Cookie Policy.

9. Sponsored Articles, Affiliate Links & Commercial Disclosures

From time to time, DPGNotes may feature curated sponsorships for academic software, student laptop discounts, university webinars, or open-source educational initiatives. In strict alignment with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255) and the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) Code for Self-Regulation:

  • Every sponsored article or promotional review is preceded by an unmistakable, bold commercial disclosure at the very top of the page.
  • All outgoing commercial hyperlinks, affiliate marketing URLs, and paid partner destinations contain mandatory rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow" HTML attributes to prevent search engine indexing bias.
  • Editorial integrity is never compromised: our syllabus reviews, study guides, and note curation processes operate completely independent of commercial sponsorships.
