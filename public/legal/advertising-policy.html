DPGNotes Comprehensive Advertising Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-ADVT-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Purpose & Standards of Platform Monetization

DPGNotes is dedicated to maintaining free, barrier-free access to high-quality academic notes, formula repositories, and syllabus guides for students worldwide. To sustain cloud hosting expenses, domain renewals, continuous security monitoring, and content delivery network (CDN) bandwidth, our platform displays carefully vetted educational advertisements and Google AdSense partner banners.

This Advertising Policy establishes clear rules regarding promotional units, user safety, and strict compliance with the Google AdSense Program Policies and the Better Ads Standards.

AdSense Certified

Strict compliance with Google Publisher Policies, no deceptive layouts, and clear disclosure markers on all promo units.

Non-Intrusive UX

No pop-unders, no forced redirects, no audio auto-play, and strict spacing that preserves document readability.

Student Safety

Zero tolerance for adult products, academic dishonesty mills, gambling, or deceptive malware downloads.

2. Permitted Ad Formats & Layout Mechanics

Promotional banners across DPGNotes are categorized into five clearly labeled formats:

  • Header Sponsored Banner: High-profile horizontal leaderboard positioned below top navigation bars, strictly demarcated with clear "Sponsored" tags.
  • Sidebar Contextual Display: Sticky or inline cards highlighting university conferences, internships, and accredited educational software tools.
  • Feed & In-Article Cards: Native format promo cards styled consistently with dark card aesthetics, strictly distinguished from genuine student lecture notes.
  • Interactive Watch-Ad Restoration Units: Optional, voluntary sponsor units that allow visitors and contributors to replenish daily generation tokens without monetary expenditure.
  • Footer Educational Banner: Bottom-of-page contextual units positioned above regulatory disclosures.

3. The Divisible-by-Three (Div-by-3) Reader Spacing Rule

To ensure student reading experiences remain focused and distraction-free in our comprehensive PDF Viewer and Model Training Hub, DPGNotes enforces a strict structural layout constraint: The Divisible-by-3 Rule.

Pages whose numbers are divisible by 3 (Page 3, Page 6, Page 9, Page 12, etc.) already host standardized Google AdSense display units. Therefore, custom native ad blocks are strictly forbidden from rendering in the "After Note" slot of any page divisible by 3. This algorithmic enforcement prevents ad stacking and guarantees optimal pedagogical immersion.

4. Strictly Prohibited Advertising Categories

To safeguard our student audience, the following advertising categories are permanently banned from DPGNotes:

  • Academic Dishonesty Services: Essay writing mills, paid ghostwriting, exam proxy testing, or term paper purchase platforms.
  • Adult & Sexually Suggestive Content: Mature themes, adult games, or dating platforms.
  • Gambling & High-Risk Speculation: Online casinos, sports betting, real-money gaming, or unregulated cryptocurrency schemes.
  • Deceptive & Malicious Software: Fake download buttons, scareware system warnings, malware, or deceptive browser extensions.
  • Tobacco, Alcohol & Controlled Substances: Any promotional messaging promoting smoking, vaping, or intoxicants.

5. Google AdSense Third-Party Cookies & Ad Settings

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on DPGNotes. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

6. User Reporting & Rapid Escalation Protocol

If you encounter an advertisement on DPGNotes that violates our content standards or appears misleading, please report it immediately to our moderation desk at dpgnotes.legal@gmail.com with the destination URL and a screenshot. Offending ad networks are blacklisted within 24 business hours.
