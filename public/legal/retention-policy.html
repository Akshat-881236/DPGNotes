DPGNotes Data Retention & Disposal Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-RETN-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Lifecycle Governance & Data Minimization

DPGNotes adheres strictly to statutory data storage limitations articulated in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023 and the GDPR. Personal information, session telemetry, and student interaction records are retained only for the duration necessary to accomplish educational purposes or fulfill statutory obligations.

This Data Retention Policy defines specific retention schedules, automated archiving processes, and permanent deletion workflows.

Defined Schedules

Clear, transparent lifespan limits for every category of user data, guest tokens, and security logs.

Self-Service Erasure

1-Click account deletion via the Danger Zone permanently purges all profile records and contributions.

Automated Pruning

Automated database scheduled routines purge expired guest tokens and transient analytics weekly.

2. Comprehensive Data Retention Schedule

Our platform enforces distinct retention schedules across six categories of data:

Data Category Retention Period Trigger Event Disposal Method
Anonymous Guest Identifiers 12 Months Last active session timestamp Automated database purge
Daily Generation Tokens 24 Hours Midnight UTC roll-over Local client key reset
Active Contributor Profiles Account Lifespan Until voluntary account deletion Hard delete from database
Published Academic Notes Indefinite / Contributor Discretion Active unless removed by author Immediate unpublishing & purge
Security Violation Audit Logs 180 Days From infraction recording date Encrypted archive overwrite
Grievance & Appeal Records 24 Months From resolution closure Secure cryptographic erasure

3. Self-Service Account Deletion (The Danger Zone)

Contributors maintain complete autonomy over their identity. Through your Contributor Dashboard → Account Settings → Danger Zone, you can trigger irreversible account termination. Executing this action initiates an atomic database deletion transaction that:

  • Permanently deletes your authenticated user profile, biographical description, and avatar reference.
  • Purges your active session authorizations, contributor badges, and leaderboard standings.
  • Grants you the option to either retain your authored academic notes under anonymous community attribution or permanently delete all uploaded notes.

4. Permanent Cryptographic Disposal Standards

When records reach the conclusion of their statutory retention lifespan or an account erasure request is processed, DPGNotes executes hard deletion protocols. Data blocks are overwritten in compliance with NIST SP 800-88 standards, ensuring that purged information cannot be reconstructed or retrieved.
