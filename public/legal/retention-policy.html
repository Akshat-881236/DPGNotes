DPGNotes Data Retention & Disposal Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026 | Document ID: POL-DPG-RET-2026-V3

1. Retention Philosophy & Legal Basis

Under the DPDP Act 2023, personal data must not be retained beyond the period necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was processed. This policy outlines our exact archival and deletion schedules.

2. Specific Data Retention Schedules

Data Classification Retention Period Disposal Mechanism
Contributor Profiles Duration of active account Purged immediately upon account self-deletion
Uploaded Study Resources Retained until author deletion Permanent removal from Cloud Firestore & Storage
Telemetry & Click Logs 180 Days Automated database scrub & archival
Suspension & Disciplinary Records 1 Year post-resolution Anonymized audit logging

3. Contributor Self-Deletion Rights ("Danger Zone")

Every registered contributor possesses the unilateral right to delete their entire account and all associated documents permanently via the Contributor Dashboard Danger Zone.
