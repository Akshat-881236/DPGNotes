DPGNotes Copyright Policy & Fair Use Guidelines

Effective Date: July 13, 2026 | Document ID: POL-DPG-COPY-2026-V3

1. Commitment to Intellectual Property

DPGNotes complies strictly with the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (as amended), the Berne Convention, and international intellectual property treaties. We actively protect legitimate educational authors while fostering academic learning.

2. Permitted Educational Fair Use (Section 52)

Under Section 52(1)(a) and Section 52(1)(i) of the Indian Copyright Act, the following academic activities are recognized as lawful fair dealing:

  • Fair dealing with any work for the purpose of private or personal use, including academic research.
  • The reproduction of excerpts by a teacher or a pupil in the course of instruction or in answers to questions.
  • Handwritten or synthesized study notes summarizing broad concepts in the student's own words.

3. Strictly Prohibited Submissions

Contributors are barred from uploading:

  • Full, unauthorized scans of published textbooks or premium commercial test series.
  • Confidential, unpublished university question papers prior to official release.
  • Proprietary courseware from private tutoring companies or copyrighted lab manuals.

4. Immediate Takedown Protocol

Upon receipt of a valid infringement notification from a verified copyright holder, DPGNotes immediately disables public access to the contested material pending review.
