DPGNotes Comprehensive Copyright Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-CPYR-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Commitment to Intellectual Property & Academic Fair Use

DPGNotes respects the intellectual property rights of authors, educators, academic publishers, and researchers. Our platform operate in strict adherence to the Copyright Act, 1957 (India) and international intellectual property treaties, including the Berne Convention and TRIPS Agreement.

Our educational repository provides non-commercial peer-to-peer study notes synthesized under statutory fair-dealing provisions.

Section 52 Fair Dealing

Synthesized study notes, formula summaries, and review cheat-sheets prepared for private study and research.

Prohibited Content

Commercial textbook PDFs, scanned publisher materials, and paid test banks are strictly banned from hosting.

Rapid Takedown SLA

Verified intellectual property infringement notices are processed and remediated within 48 business hours.

2. Statutory Fair Dealing under Indian Law

In accordance with Section 52(1)(a)(i) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the following educational acts do not constitute an infringement of copyright:

  • A fair dealing with any work for the purpose of private or personal use, including educational research and study.
  • The making of synthesized lecture notes, student revision guides, and summary outlines by scholars for peer learning.
  • The quotation and academic citation of brief excerpts accompanied by clear identification of the original author and publication source.

3. Contributor Guidelines for Copyright Compliance

Contributors must strictly observe the following rules prior to publishing materials on DPGNotes:

  • Original Authorship: Study notes must represent original synthesis, personal summaries, and explanatory prose created by the contributor.
  • No Scanned Commercial Volumes: Do not upload verbatim scans or digital copies of commercially available textbooks, reference handbooks, or copyrighted question banks.
  • Attribution Requirement: When drawing definitions, formulas, or academic diagrams from textbooks, proper academic attribution must be included.

4. Copyright Infringement Notice & Removal Protocol

If you are a copyright holder or an authorized licensing representative who believes that material hosted on DPGNotes infringes upon your exclusive copyright, please submit a formal notification containing:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, including title, ISBN, or online source URL.
  • The exact URL or resource ID on DPGNotes where the allegedly infringing material is hosted.
  • Your contact details, including full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and official email address.
  • A statement of good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or statutory law.
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

Submit all infringement notices to our designated agent at dpgnotes.legal@gmail.com. Removals are executed within 48 hours of verification.
