DPGNotes Legal & Academic Disclaimer

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-DISC-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Academic Collaboration & Educational Purpose

DPGNotes is an open, student-led academic collaboration platform operated under Akshat Network Hub. All lecture notes, formula cheat-sheets, syllabus breakdowns, and examination review guides shared on this portal are prepared voluntarily by student contributors and academic volunteers solely for non-commercial educational assistance.

This comprehensive Disclaimer articulates the boundaries of our hosted educational materials and clarifies institutional affiliations.

Peer Summaries

Materials reflect student lecture notes and peer summaries, not official university-authorized textbooks or grading rubrics.

Non-Affiliation

No official institutional sponsorship or endorsement by universities, government departments, or commercial publishers.

Independent Verification

Students must cross-check revision notes against official syllabus guidelines published by their respective universities.

2. Syllabus Discrepancies & Independent Cross-Verification

University academic curricula, course syllabi, recommended textbook editions, and grading structures undergo periodic revisions across academic sessions and university boards (including Maharshi Dayanand University Rohtak, Gurugram University, Kurukshetra University, and Delhi University).

While our student editorial team and automated verification algorithms strive to ensure academic fidelity, DPGNotes cannot guarantee that hosted notes reflect recent mid-semester syllabus amendments. Users are strongly urged to cross-verify all study notes against official university notifications, department handouts, and prescribed textbooks before examinations.

3. No Institutional Affiliation or Official Endorsement

Reference to any university, college, educational board, professor, department head, or commercial textbook publisher on DPGNotes is made strictly for descriptive, educational, and categorization purposes. Such references do not imply or constitute official endorsement, institutional sponsorship, or contractual partnership between DPGNotes and said entities, unless explicitly articulated.

4. AI Knowledge Assistant & Fact-Checking Disclaimer

Our platform incorporates artificial intelligence tools for real-time tag standardization, spelling verification, and academic content assistance. While these algorithms utilize modern natural language processing, automated suggestions may occasionally contain factual nuances or typographical variations. Students must exercise scholarly discernment before incorporating automated suggestions into academic submissions.

5. External Links & Third-Party Endpoints

DPGNotes contains outbound hyperlinks to external academic endpoints, including DigiIndia Student Search Engine, YouTube video lectures, GitHub source repositories, and cloud document drives. DPGNotes holds no control over, and assumes no liability for, the content, privacy standards, or uptime of third-party web destinations.

6. Limitation of Academic Liability

Under no circumstances shall DPGNotes, Akshat Network Hub, its developers, or its student contributors be held liable for any academic outcomes, loss of marks, exam disqualifications, or scholastic consequences resulting from reliance on notes hosted on this portal.
