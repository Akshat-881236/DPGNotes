DPGNotes Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-PRIV-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Preamble & Scope of Governance

This Comprehensive Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and retention of information by DPGNotes (an open student academic collaboration portal operated under Akshat Network Hub). We are firmly dedicated to preserving the digital privacy of every student, teacher, researcher, and educational volunteer who visits or interacts with our study repository.

Our data governance practices comply strictly with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023 (India), the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

DPDP Act 2023

Strict lawful purpose limitation, explicit affirmative consent, and comprehensive data principal rights under Indian statutory law.

GDPR & CCPA

Global privacy parity offering EU and California users right to access, right to rectification, and right to complete digital erasure.

Zero Commercial Sale

We pledge unconditionally that student data, email addresses, and reading habits are never commercialized, rented, or brokered.

2. Information We Collect & Collection Modalities

DPGNotes follows a strict data minimization doctrine. We collect only the minimum quantum of data necessary to provide academic services, track daily fair-use generation limits, and safeguard the integrity of collaborative study notes.

  • Anonymous Guest Identifiers: Randomly generated cryptographic tokens stored locally in your browser to maintain daily fair-use quotas (such as assignment cover page downloads) without collecting identifying personal profiles.
  • Authenticated Contributor Data: When you voluntarily register or log in via verified federated OAuth providers (such as Google Identity), we receive your basic public profile, including your verified email address, full display name, and avatar image. We do not obtain, store, or process your external account passwords.
  • Academic Contribution Records: Digital notes, revision cheat-sheets, syllabus breakdowns, examination papers, and tags submitted by contributors. These contributions are published under academic community guidelines with public contributor attribution.
  • Device & Interaction Telemetry: Anonymized HTTP network logs, approximate non-granular geolocation (country and state level only), browser user-agent tokens, referring search parameters, and interface interaction metrics used solely for performance optimization and abuse defense.

3. Purpose of Data Processing & Legal Grounds

Every processing activity conducted by DPGNotes is grounded in legitimate educational interests or explicit user consent under Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023 and Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • To render, format, and deliver interactive lecture notes and PDF academic previews.
  • To enable contributors to author, revise, and categorize curriculum-aligned study materials.
  • To compute anonymous community engagement metrics (such as note likes and verified shares).
  • To prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) disruptions, automated scraping, and unauthorized attempts to breach database nodes.
  • To display contextual educational partner banners and Google AdSense advertisements compliant with strict publisher standards.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising Disclosures

DPGNotes displays contextual learning advertisements and collaborates with vetted technology partners. In compliance with Google AdSense Publisher Policies:

  • Third-party ad vendors, including Google, employ cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our platform or other educational websites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out portal.
  • We enforce strict content safety guidelines: advertisements depicting gambling, adult themes, deceptive academic dishonesty services, or malicious download redirects are prohibited and automatically blocked.

5. Data Storage, Cryptographic Protection & Cloud Architecture

All user records, security violation logs, and published study resources are stored in encrypted cloud database infrastructure with multi-zone redundancy. Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 cryptographic tunnels, and data at rest is secured via AES-256 bit hardware-accelerated encryption.

Database access privileges are restricted via role-based access controls (RBAC) and automated validation rules. Administrative endpoints require cryptographically signed tokens with rotating secrets, preventing unauthorized database inspection.

6. User Rights & Data Principal Privileges

Regardless of your geographic location, DPGNotes grants you comprehensive sovereignty over your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Review: You may request an export of all personal data, contribution logs, and profile records linked to your account.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: Contributors can update inaccurate academic records, biographical descriptions, and subject tags directly through their profile dashboard.
  • Right to Erasure (The Right to Be Forgotten): You may permanently delete your account and all associated documents using the self-service Danger Zone panel or by submitting a formal erasure request to our Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for optional analytics or telemetry processing at any time through your browser settings without affecting core document reading features.

7. Protection of Minors & Academic Age Gate

DPGNotes is an academic resource designed for university students, college scholars, and high school learners. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental or institutional consent, in adherence with COPPA and Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023.
