DPGNotes Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026 | Document ID: POL-DPG-PRIV-2026-V3

At DPGNotes ("we", "us", "our", or the "Platform"), operated in association with Akshat Network Hub, we consider the privacy, confidentiality, and autonomy of our student community, academic contributors, and faculty visitors to be of paramount importance. This Comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines our data governance standards in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and relevant international privacy benchmarks (GDPR & COPPA).

1. Core Regulatory Principles & Scope

This policy applies to all visitors accessing DPGNotes via our official web interfaces (including dpgnotes.web.app, dpgnotes.firebaseapp.com, and affiliated subdomains). We collect and process personal data strictly adhering to three foundational principles:

  • Lawfulness & Fairness: Processing occurs solely upon valid consent or lawful academic mandate.
  • Purpose Limitation: Collected data is restricted to educational delivery, note contribution indexing, and anti-abuse security enforcement.
  • Data Minimization: We never request sensitive biological, financial, or political information; anonymous access is supported for general PDF reading.

2. Categories of Data We Collect

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Contributor Account Identity: Full Name, institutional email address, Google Profile picture (via Google OAuth 2.0 / Firebase Authentication), academic discipline, and college affiliation.
  • Uploaded Academic Resources: Document titles, subject descriptions, semester classifications, syllabus tags, custom in-document notes, and syllabus formula contributions.
  • Support & Appeal Communications: Correspondence submitted via the Suspension Support Contact Form or Admin Grievance channels.

B. Telemetry & Automatically Collected Information

  • Anonymous Device Telemetry: Device category (mobile/tablet/desktop), browser type and version, viewport screen resolution, and operating system.
  • Interaction Logs: PDF page scroll progress, search queries entered, like/unlike toggles on contributor notes, and short share link redirection tokens.
  • Local Storage State: Dark/light UI mode, active session tokens, and daily unauthenticated access quota metrics (6 page visits / 3 PDF reads per 24 hours).

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed strictly for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. To authenticate academic contributors and grant upload/editing privileges for revision notes and assignments.
  2. To power the AI Notes Assistant and search indexers, providing relevant academic summaries and formula insights.
  3. To compile aggregate contributor engagement metrics (like counts, share generation telemetry, and reading engagement).
  4. To enforce fair usage quotas and prevent malicious automated scraping under the DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA).

4. Security Architecture & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to marketing brokers. All database records are hosted on Google Cloud Firebase Firestore in encrypted infrastructure (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit). Access is controlled through verified Firebase Security Rules and backend JWT authorization.

5. Rights of the Data Principal (Your Rights)

Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023, you are entitled to:

  • Right to Access: Request a full export of your profile information and uploaded materials.
  • Right to Correction: Edit your contributor profile, update previously published in-document notes, and refine document tags.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): Permanently delete your account and all associated documents directly via the Contributor Dashboard Danger Zone.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Submit formal privacy complaints to our designated Data Protection Officer.

6. Data Protection Officer (DPO) & Grievance Redressal

In compliance with Indian IT Rules, our designated Grievance Officer details are:

Designation: Data Protection & Grievance Officer, DPGNotes

Entity: Akshat Network Hub / DPGNotes Academic Operations

Grievance Escalation: Official Support & Grievance Portal

Response SLA: All formal privacy inquiries are reviewed and resolved within 7 business days.
