DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA)

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-DRAS-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Statutory Mission & Regulatory Scope

The DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA) represents our statutory governance framework designed to maintain academic integrity, protect contributor rights, and prevent malicious exploitation of collaborative student tools across the DPGNotes platform.

DRASA applies uniformly to all platform participants, including anonymous guests, verified student contributors, academic peer reviewers, and platform moderators.

Proactive Defense

Automated threat detection and peer audits designed to maintain a trustworthy academic repository.

5-Tier Violation Matrix

Proportional disciplinary escalation ranging from formal warnings to permanent IP and network blacklisting.

Due Process & Appeals

Transparent investigation protocols with guaranteed right of appeal to an impartial Grievance Officer.

2. Comprehensive Five-Tier Violation Hierarchy

Violations under DRASA are categorized into five progressive tiers based on severity, intentionality, and systemic risk:

  • Tier 1 — Administrative Non-Compliance: Inadvertent formatting errors, duplicate tag creation, or minor typographical non-compliance. Sanction: Automated notification, formatting correction, and formal advisory.
  • Tier 2 — Content Quality & Attribution Infractions: Uploading low-fidelity notes, unverified formula summaries, or failing to properly cite referenced open-access lecture materials. Sanction: Temporary resource delisting pending revision; 7-day contributor account probation.
  • Tier 3 — Referral Fraud & Quota Exploitation: Forging invite tokens, deploying automated click scripts, or attempting to bypass daily generation quotas through synthetic identities. Sanction: 14-day account suspension, forfeiture of referral badges, and quota throttling.
  • Tier 4 — Copyright Infringement & Intellectual Property Violations: Uploading commercially copyrighted textbooks, paid exam test-banks, or infringing third-party proprietary academic works. Sanction: Immediate permanent document deletion, 30-day account suspension, and formal copyright advisory.
  • Tier 5 — Security Breaches & Malicious Tampering: Attempting database payload injections, cross-site scripting (XSS), credential phishing, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS), or unauthorized database tampering. Sanction: Permanent account termination, cryptographic IP ban, and reporting to relevant statutory cyber authorities under the Indian Information Technology Act 2000.

3. Investigation Procedures & Due Process

Upon detection of an infraction (either via automated telemetry or community user reports), the platform administration executes an impartial review process:

  • Evidence gathering: Cryptographic audit logs, content diffs, and network telemetry are recorded securely.
  • Notification: The account holder receives an electronic notice detailing the alleged infraction, affected documents, and applicable DRASA tier.
  • Interim measures: Potentially infringing or harmful documents are quarantined from public discovery during active review.
  • Determination: Findings are published within 7 business days with transparent rationale.

4. Formal Appeals & Restoration Protocol

Any participant whose account or contribution has been subject to disciplinary sanctions under DRASA holds the statutory right to appeal within 15 calendar days of sanction notification. Appeals must be submitted through our Official Appeals Portal or emailed directly to dpgnotes.legal@gmail.com with supporting academic evidence. Decisions by the Grievance Review Panel are finalized within 10 business days.
