DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA)

Effective Date: July 13, 2026 | Document ID: ACT-DPG-DRASA-2026-V3

1. Legislative Purpose & Jurisdiction

The DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA) serves as the primary governance code of DPGNotes. Enacted to preserve scholarly integrity, deter fraudulent traffic inflation, and protect platform safety, DRASA applies universally to all visitors, contributors, and administrators.

2. The Five-Tier Disciplinary Classification

Level I — Informational & Formatting Guidance

Minor formatting defects, missing tags, or accidental duplicate uploads. Outcome: Educational guidance and automated tag suggestions.

Level II — Minor Infractions

Low-effort spam submissions, non-academic uploads, or repeated category mismatches. Outcome: Formal warning badge and mandatory review for subsequent uploads.

Level III — Moderate Violations

Share link spamming, automated script abuse, or misleading document titles. Outcome: Temporary 7-day contributor suspension and revocation of share generation privileges.

Level IV — Referral Fraud & Serious Copyright Piracy

Uploading commercial scanned textbooks, fabricating referral invitations, or generating fake engagement clicks.
Referral Fraud Penalty Escalation:
• 1st Offense: 5-day account suspension & referral points forfeiture.
• 2nd Offense: 12-day account suspension & full contributor demotion.
• 3rd Offense: Permanent account termination and hardware fingerprint ban.

Level V — Critical Security & Malicious Tampering

SQL injection, API exploitation, credential theft, cross-site scripting, or illegal content hosting. Outcome: Immediate, permanent IP and Google account termination with legal escalation.

3. Unauthenticated Guest Access Quota Architecture

To thwart aggressive bot scrapers while maintaining open education, guest users are allocated a strict daily quota of 6 page visits and 3 PDF reads per 24 hours. Quotas reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Legal policy documentation is permanently exempt from quota restrictions.

4. Official Appeal & Grievance Mechanism

Suspended contributors who believe their suspension was issued in error may file an official appeal through the Support & Suspension Appeal Portal. Appeals are independently audited within 7 business days.
