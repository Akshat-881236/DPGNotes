Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DPGNotes ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with your use of our platform. We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with modern privacy frameworks, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Information Collection

We collect credentials and information to provide better services to our users. This includes account profile information (names, emails) and resource uploads (notes, exams, sample papers).

2. Information Security

All data is hosted securely in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore. We enforce strict Firebase Security Rules, restricting read/write permissions to verified account uploaders and administrators. Sensitive transactions are processed behind JWT token authorization headers.

Terms & Conditions

Last Revised: July 13, 2026

By accessing or uploading materials to DPGNotes, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms & Conditions of use. Please review them carefully.

1. Platform Use & Account Eligibility

DPGNotes is designed as an open educational repository. Users may register to upload academic notes, sample papers, sessional guides, and tutorials. Contributors retain the right to delete their own uploaded documents as well as delete their entire account profile via the dashboard Danger Zone at any time.

2. Administration Rights

Administrators have the authority to suspend or permanently block user profiles found violating sessional content standards or copyright guidelines. Suspended contributors are barred from uploading or generating share links for the suspension duration. Administrators can also permanently delete generated share codes, terminating access for visitors and sending automated email warnings to all interested parties.

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

DPGNotes uses cookies, local storage key-values, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your interface interaction and persist session state preferences.

1. What Technologies We Use

  • Local Storage: Used to persist theme preferences (dark/light mode), secure admin JWT tokens, and contributor session states.
  • Firebase Authentication Cookies: Firebase Auth SDK utilizes browser tokens to persist logged-in statuses securely.

2. Consent Management

By using DPGNotes, you consent to the storage of essential session tokens necessary for account authorization, security, and theme preferences.

DMCA Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), we have established a take-down procedure to address complaints about copyright violations on our platform.

1. Submitting a Take-Down Request

If you believe that any material residing on DPGNotes infringes your copyright, you may submit a formal notification containing:

  • Your legal signature and contact details.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  • The exact URL (e.g. share code or document ID) hosting the material.
Requests should be sent directly via our Contact form.

Tracking & Analytics Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

We collect essential analytics to improve site load speeds, monitor security anomalies, and track academic resource engagement (Click Through Rate / CTR).

1. Telemetry for Guest Users

When you visit the site anonymously, the platform may track:

  • Anonymous Guest ID / Session ID
  • Timestamps, document views, and PDF download counts
  • Operating system, browser agent version, device type, and screen resolution
  • Search queries and custom theme selections

2. Telemetry for Authenticated Contributors

For logged-in users, the platform records:

  • User ID, Username, Role, and masked Email address
  • Detailed action logs (uploads, likes, shares, login/logout timestamps)
  • Document click-through records mapping back to the generating token

Security Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

We utilize best-in-class security measures to protect contributor uploads and administrative accounts from unauthorized access.

1. Server Security

All database queries undergo validation via Google Firebase server-side Firestore Rules. Admin actions require JWT authentication tokens verified on the Render server.

2. API Security

Our backend APIs employ CORS protection and JWT token verification. Deletions and user modifications are strictly logged in the Activity Logs database.

Data Retention Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

We only retain collected personal data and document records for as long as necessary to fulfill academic usage and compliance mandates.

1. Contributor Deletion

When a contributor deletes their account, all associated documents, user logs, and database profiles are permanently and immediately deleted from Firestore.

2. Activity and Traffic Logs

Click engagements, telemetry logs, and activity entries are archived or scrubbed after 180 days to minimize data footprint.

DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA)

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

The DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA) establishes the official governance framework for DPGNotes. It defines conduct standards, uploader responsibilities, and administrative enforcement procedures including temporary suspensions, long-term bans, appeal procedures, and disciplinary metrics.

2. Disciplinary Classifications

Violations on the portal are categorized from Level I to Level V:

  • Level I (Informational): Minor duplicate uploads or formatting errors resulting in educational guidance.
  • Level II (Minor): Low quality submissions or spam resulting in warning notices.
  • Level III (Moderate): Repeated category errors or share link abuse resulting in temporary contributor suspension.
  • Level IV (Serious): Direct copyright infringement or uploading commercial textbook PDFs without license, leading to 15 to 90 days suspension.
  • Level V (Critical Security): Phishing, credential scraping, database tampering, or host infrastructure attack attempts resulting in immediate, permanent ban and account deletion.

Legal FAQ

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

Can I delete notes that I uploaded to the portal?

Yes. Contributors have full ownership and rights to edit or permanently delete any resources they uploaded directly from their Contributor Dashboard.

What happens if a share link I generated is deleted by an admin?

If an administrator terminates a share link, the link becomes invalid and redirects to DPGNotes homepage. An automated email notice is sent to you (the generator) and any regular visitors logged in our database.
