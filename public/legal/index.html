1. Introduction & Scope

The DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA) establishes the official governance framework for DPGNotes. It defines conduct standards, uploader responsibilities, and administrative enforcement procedures including temporary suspensions, long-term bans, appeal procedures, and disciplinary metrics.

2. Disciplinary Classifications & Referral Fraud Penalties

Violations on the portal are categorized from Level I to Level V:

  • Level I (Informational): Minor duplicate uploads or formatting errors resulting in educational guidance.
  • Level II (Minor): Low quality submissions or spam resulting in warning notices.
  • Level III (Moderate): Repeated category errors or share link abuse resulting in temporary contributor suspension.
  • Level IV (Serious & Referral Fraud): Direct copyright infringement, or Referral Fraud (inviting existing users or generating mismatching invitation codes). Referral Fraud Penalties: 1st Offense = 5-day suspension; 2nd Offense = 12-day suspension; 3rd+ Offense = Permanent Account Ban.
  • Level V (Critical Security): Phishing, credential scraping, database tampering, or host infrastructure attack attempts resulting in immediate, permanent ban and account deletion.

3. Anonymous Guest Access Quota Rules

To preserve infrastructure stability and prevent scraping, unauthenticated guest users are granted a daily quota of 6 page visits and 3 PDF reads per 24 hours. Exceeding this quota requires signing up with a valid Google account to continue accessing academic materials. Legal Policy pages remain unconditionally accessible.

DPGNotes employs a dual-interaction monetization architecture designed to reward authentic user engagement while penalizing synthetic, accidental, or deceptive clicks:

  • Genuine Hover Rate: ₹24 per unique hover / month for verified contributors, and ₹12 (50% reduced rate) for guest visitors. Calculated strictly for authentic cursor dwell events lasting at least 5 continuous seconds (5000ms) on the ad destination button. Accidental glides, quick passes, and robotic scripts are excluded.
  • Genuine Visit Rate: ₹40 per unique visit / month for verified contributors, and ₹20 (50% reduced rate) for guest visitors. Validated when a user navigates via the Safe Destination Gateway (/ads-redirect-handler.html) opened from the DPGNotes platform in the same tab. Direct URL navigation is classified non-billable.
  • Strict Single Engagement per Ad per Month Rule: For each unique verified user or guest visitor, exactly one billable engagement (hover OR click, whichever occurs earlier) per ad campaign per calendar month is recorded in Firestore. Any subsequent interaction for the same ad from the same visitor within the same monthly cycle is rejected and never billed.
  • Strict Self-Interaction Exclusion: Hovers and clicks by active contributors on their own uploaded ads (or ads uploaded by linked duplicate accounts) are 100% non-billable and bypassed automatically.
  • AI Profile Check & Identity Clustering: An AI algorithm cross-references registered accounts across email variations, names, GitHub, LinkedIn, and Settings bio details. All duplicate accounts within a cluster are treated as a single entity; only one interaction across the entire cluster is valid per ad per month.

Quiz Examination & Candidate Telemetry Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • Reference: POL-DPG-QUIZ-EVAL-2026-V1.0

Comprehensive Quiz Policy & Analytics Informatory Available

Read all complete clauses, anti-cheat UMC criteria, candidate ranking metrics, and answer key release rules.

Read Dedicated Quiz Policy

This Quiz Examination Policy governs all online academic assessments, candidate scoring, proctoring telemetry, answer key disclosures, and contributor authoring standards conducted on the DPGNotes portal.

1. Candidate Identity Verification & Single Attempt Rule

To preserve institutional integrity and academic fairness, every examination enforces a strict single-attempt restriction. Re-submission or retaking a completed assessment is strictly prohibited. The system validates and binds candidate submissions across three independent integrity factors:

  • Verified Student ID: Each submission must contain a unique Student / Roll Number ID verified against existing attempts for the quiz.
  • Authenticated Account / Guest UID: Both verified contributor sessions and guest Firebase Authentication sessions are permanently bound to their attempt record in Firestore.
  • Workstation Session Lock: Browser local storage tokens record completed attempts to prevent unauthorized re-entry on the same client.

2. Anti-Cheat Monitoring & UMC Telemetry Engine

Assessments conducted under proctored or strict mode actively record academic integrity telemetry using modern browser security APIs:

  • Tab Switching & Visibility Change: When a candidate navigates away from the examination window or switches tabs, a TAB_OR_APP_HIDDEN violation flag is logged. Under strict mode, tab abandonment triggers automatic immediate assessment submission.
  • Fullscreen Compliance: Assessments configured for proctored mode require an uninterrupted fullscreen view. Exiting fullscreen mode registers a FULLSCREEN_EXIT integrity infraction.
  • Clipboard & Context Menu Protection: Right-clicking, copying questions, and pasting unauthorized text are automatically intercepted and recorded as telemetry flags.

3. Cryptographic Answer Key Privacy & Deferred Evaluation

To prevent answer extraction or inspect-element tampering during active examinations, public quiz payloads contain only question prompts and options. Correct answer keys are stored in a contributor-only restricted document. Authors configure assessments under one of two disclosure models:

  • Immediate Result Declaration: Candidates receive instant scoring, percentage calculations, and response breakdowns upon submission.
  • Deferred Evaluation: Candidate responses are securely submitted in a PENDING evaluation state. Official scorecards and ranking metrics are declared only when the verified author officially releases the answer key.

4. Deterministic Scoring & Ranking Computations

Scores are calculated deterministically using the author's configured parameters: designated score per correct response, optional negative marks for incorrect options, and unattempted question penalties. Candidate ranks are evaluated dynamically based on final score, with ties resolved deterministically by submission duration (shorter durations achieve higher priority) and zero UMC infractions.

5. Contributor Authoring & Content Safety
