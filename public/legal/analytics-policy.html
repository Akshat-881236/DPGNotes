DPGNotes Tracking & Analytics Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 Reference: POL-DPG-ANLY-2026-V3.2 Version: 3.2 (Comprehensive Edition)
Looking for a quick executive summary? Visit the Summary Version of this Policy in Legal Hub.

1. Principles of Educational Telemetry

DPGNotes believes that academic tools should empower students without subjecting them to intrusive surveillance or behavioral commercial profiling. This Tracking & Analytics Policy transparently explains our telemetry methodologies, analytical metrics, and privacy safeguards.

Our analytics infrastructure operates in compliance with the DPDP Act 2023, the ePrivacy Directive, and the principle of data minimization.

Zero PII Telemetry

We do not collect names, contact numbers, exact GPS coordinates, or private browsing histories in our analytical feeds.

Ephemeral Tokens

Anonymous visitor tokens rotate periodically and are never mapped to real-world government identities.

Academic Insights

Telemetry serves exclusively to optimize popular syllabus searches, document rendering speeds, and platform uptime.

2. Specific Metrics Collected & Analytical Objectives

DPGNotes logs limited, aggregated telemetry events strictly for service improvement and infrastructure scalability:

  • Document Engagement Counts: Aggregate page views, PDF viewer reading duration, and download requests across semester courses. This reveals which syllabi require updated notes.
  • Query Telemetry: Anonymized internal search queries entered in our DPGNotes Search Engine and SERP pages, used to identify missing subject topics and improve search indexing.
  • Daily Fair-Use Quota Counters: Ephemeral daily counters stored in browser LocalStorage to verify compliance with visitor generation limits.
  • Sponsor Banner Interaction CTR: Aggregated, non-personalized click-through rates (CTR) on educational partner banners to measure ad performance for server funding.
  • Performance & Error Telemetry: Client-side JavaScript errors, canvas rendering latencies, and network timeout logs used by our engineering volunteers to fix bugs.

3. Anonymous Guest Identifiers & Storage Mechanisms

When you visit DPGNotes without logging in, our system generates an anonymous randomized token stored locally in your browser (e.g., dpg_guest_id). This token does not contain personal data, cannot identify your physical location, and exists solely to ensure fair distribution of cloud resources across our student user base.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Google Analytics Safeguards

Where Google Analytics is utilized to assess aggregate traffic patterns, we enforce strict privacy enhancements:

  • IP Anonymization: IP addresses are automatically masked and truncated at the earliest ingestion stage.
  • Disabled Advertising Demographics: We disable Google Signals cross-device tracking and behavioral advertising demographics.
  • Zero Data Sharing: Data sharing settings with Google benchmarking services and third-party advertising brokers are turned off.

5. Opt-Out Rights & Browser Privacy Controls

You maintain the right to prevent analytical logging at any time. You may install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, enable the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, or activate "Do Not Track" in your browser settings. DPGNotes respects client-side privacy signals without degrading your ability to read study documents.
