Examinations

Sessional & University Exam PYQ Methodology

Scoring high marks in university degree examinations requires understanding question frequency and internal sessional patterns. Our archive provides actual question papers collected across departments.

Structured Answering Strategies:

  • Internal Sessional Tests (SE): Focus heavily on mid-unit algorithms, block diagrams, and mathematical proofs. Midterms represent 20% of your total internal evaluation.
  • University Final Exams (UE): Past 5-year PYQs reveal that approximately 65% of questions recur with slight numerical or architectural modifications.
  • Formatting Recommendation: Always draw annotated block diagrams before starting lengthy text answers, especially in subjects like Computer Architecture, DBMS, and Networking.

DPGNotes operates under the DPGNotes Regulations & Suspension Act (DRASA). Every contributor is committed to maintaining high academic standards and respecting copyright policies.
